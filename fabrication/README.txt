arpeggiator

220 x 170 mm, four layers, 1.6 mm FR4.
1 oz finished outer copper, 0.5 oz inner copper. Both inner layers are ground planes.
Green solder mask, white silkscreen, lead-free HASL.
0.127 mm minimum tracks and clearance.
All 1127 through vias have 0.30 mm drills.
Smallest via pad is 0.48 mm. Component holes and slots are unchanged.
Layer order: F_Cu, In1_Cu, In2_Cu, B_Cu.
No controlled impedance.
Separate PTH and NPTH Excellon files are included. Plated slots use routed Excellon commands.
Follow the supplied solder-mask files around vias. Flag any mask or plugging changes for review.

One bottom-side frameless stencil, 0.12 mm thick.
Use arpeggiator-B_Paste.gbp. Prepare it for printing the underside of the board.
The twelve front LEDs will be hand soldered.
